# PropDesk marketing graphics

Campaign pack for [PropDesk](https://propdesk-beta.vercel.app/).

Two desks. Two audiences. Same blotter.

| Folder | Who it talks to |
|---|---|
| `traders/` | People about to buy an evaluation or instant book |
| `firms/` | Prop firms who **pay to get listed** |
| `stills/` | Photoreal plates for YouTube / TikTok b-roll |

Each audience has `x/` (1600×900), `tiktok/` (1080×1920), and `youtube/` (1280×720 thumbs + banner).

Brand lock: blotter `#231F19`, paper `#EFE6D4`, ink `#161310`. Source Serif 4 headline. Ticket-P. No neon. No trophies. No "best firm." No other company logos as endorsement.

Paid listing is disclosed on every firm creative. Listing is coverage, not a rank.

## YouTube series — traders

**Open Book Live** — live trading by people who understand the rules.

Hook: the chart is open, the rule book is open, the size is last.

1. Live with the rule book open — `traders/youtube/thumb-live-rule-book-open.jpg`
2. The homepage never said this — `thumb-homepage-never-said.jpg`
3. Stop. Read drawdown. Then size — `thumb-stop-read-drawdown.jpg`
4. Passed the eval. Still failed — `thumb-passed-eval-still-failed.jpg`
5. Discord is not the source — `thumb-discord-not-source.jpg`

Channel art: `traders/youtube/channel-banner.jpg`

Do not promise payouts. Do not crown a firm.
