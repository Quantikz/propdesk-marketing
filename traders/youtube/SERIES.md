# Open Book Live

Viral format for the trader channel. Not a signal room. Not a payout flex.

## Promise

Live trading by people who understand the rules.

The chart is open. The rule book is open. Size comes last.

## Episode spine (repeat every week)

1. Cold open (3s): failed rule on screen, no face required.
2. Title card: Open Book Live · the rule of the hour.
3. Open the official page. Read the line out loud.
4. Open PropDesk. Show the explain card: short answer, official, example, mistake, last verified.
5. Trade small or stand down. Narrate the constraint, not the heroics.
6. End card: Understand the rules before you buy. propdesk-beta.vercel.app
