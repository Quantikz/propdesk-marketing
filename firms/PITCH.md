# Firm listing pitch

Firms pay to get listed. Listing is coverage of their official rules. It is not a rank, a partner badge, or a payout guarantee.

## Who this is for

Head of growth / compliance at a prop firm whose traders already Google the rules before checkout.

## Offer line

Traders check the book before they pay you. Get listed on the desk they open first.

## What they buy

- A firm sheet: Overview, Rules, Breaches, Payouts, Restrictions, FAQ
- Explain cards quoted from their official page, with last-verified dates
- A row on the compare table (facts only)
- Disclosure: Paid listing

## What they do not buy

- A score
- A "best for scalping" badge
- Control of trust pills (verified only when the live page is quoted)
- Removal of trader-reported payout boards (those stay on a different shelf)
